UPC / GTIN implementation

Prototype status: Every product above has a unique 12-digit, UPC-A-formatted identifier with a mathematically valid check digit. These numbers are intentionally marked as prototype UPCs. They are not GS1-issued GTINs and should not be submitted to retailers or marketplaces as official UPCs.

Before launch, obtain the appropriate GS1 company identification and assign one GTIN to each sellable trade item/variant. If size is treated as a separate trade item, each size/color combination may require its own GTIN.
